  ### 1. Best for Geopolitics & Politics                                                                                       
                                                                                                                               
  #### TheNewsAPI.com https://www.thenewsapi.com (Top Recommendation for Budget Projects)                                      
                                                                                                                               
  • Why it's great: Unlike most providers, TheNewsAPI allows commercial use across its tiers. It pulls from 40,000+ sources    
  across 50+ countries and has extensive category filters for politics, business, and global events.                           
  • Pricing:                                                                                                                   
      • Free: 100 requests/day, 3 articles per request.                                                                        
      • Basic: $19/month (or ~$16/mo billed annually) for 2,500 requests/day and 25 articles per request.                      
  • Best use: Displaying curated headlines, summaries, and source attribution for politics and world affairs.                  
                                                                                                                               
  #### Currents API https://currentsapi.services                                                                               
                                                                                                                               
  • Why it's great: Explicitly allows commercial use on its free tier without requiring a credit card upfront. Covers 20,000+  
  sources in 160+ countries and has dedicated geopolitical and political topic filters.                                        
  • Pricing:                                                                                                                   
      • Free: 250 requests/day, 20 results per request, 30-day history lookback.                                               
      • Builder: $69/month for 2,500 requests/day, 6-month historical depth.                                                   
  • Best use: High-frequency headline streams and automated feeds.                                                             
                                                                                                                               
  #### World News API https://worldnewsapi.com                                                                                 
                                                                                                                               
  • Why it's great: Built specifically for text mining, sentiment extraction, and semantic search. It monitors front pages of  
  major newspapers worldwide and tags geopolitical entities and locations automatically.                                       
  • Pricing:                                                                                                                   
      • Free: 50 points/day (~1,500 requests/month).                                                                           
      • Starter: $9 to $29/month for expanded daily points.                                                                    
  • Best use: Deep entity search (e.g., search news specifically tagging a country, leader, or conflict).

  ### 2. Best for Stocks & Finance                                                                                             
                                                                                                                               
  #### Marketaux https://www.marketaux.com (Top Recommendation for Finance)                                                    
                                                                                                                               
  • Why it's great: Purpose-built for financial and market news. It automatically parses articles and tags them by stock       
  tickers (e.g., $AAPL, $NVDA), financial entities, and market sentiment (bullish/bearish).                                    
  • Coverage: 5,000+ financial media sources across 80+ global exchanges.                                                      
  • Pricing:                                                                                                                   
      • Free: 100 requests/day (useful for initial dev/testing).                                                               
      • Basic: $24–$29/month for 2,500 requests/day and 20 articles per request.                                               
  • Best use: Building financial dashboards, stock tracking apps, or investor alert feeds.                                     
                                                                                                                               
  #### Alpha Vantage https://www.alphavantage.co                                                                               
                                                                                                                               
  • Why it's great: Widely recognized market data provider with a dedicated Market News & Sentiment API endpoint. You can query
  news filtered by ticker, topic (e.g., financial_markets, economy_macro), or sentiment score.                                 
  • Pricing:                                                                                                                   
      • Free: 25 requests/day.                                                                                                 
      • Premium: Starts at $24.99/month for 75 API calls/minute and full commercial rights.                                    
  • Best use: Combining real-time financial news with stock price charts and technical indicators.                             
                                                                                                                               
  #### Financial Modeling Prep (FMP) https://financialmodelingprep.com                                                         
                                                                                                                               
  • Why it's great: Offers a unified API for stock financial statements, SEC filings, analyst ratings, and press releases      
  alongside stock news (/stable/news).                                                                                         
  • Pricing: Free tier available; starter commercial plan begins around $19–$29/month.

  ### 3. Popular APIs to Avoid for Cheap Commercial Use                                                                        
                                                                                                                               
  • NewsAPI.org: Great API, but their free tier is strictly limited to localhost testing. A production commercial license      
  starts at $449/month.                                                                                                        
  • Finnhub.io: Outstanding stock news API, but their free tier terms explicitly mandate non-commercial/personal use only.     
  Commercial redistribution requires custom licensing.                                                                         
  • Mediastack: Free tier strictly forbids commercial use and doesn't support HTTPS. Commercial plans start at $24.99/month for
  10,000 calls/month.                                                                                                          
  • Google News RSS Scraping: While free to pull via RSS, Google's Terms of Service prohibit scraping/redistribution in        
  commercial products, and automated servers frequently get blocked with IP CAPTCHAs.

  ### 4. How to Keep Your Costs at $0 to $30/Month                                                                             
                                                                                                                               
  1. Implement Server-Side Caching (Redis / MemoryCache):                                                                      
      • News does not change by the millisecond. Cache API responses on your backend for 5 to 15 minutes.                      
      • If 1,000 users visit your app in an hour, you only make 4 to 12 API calls to the provider instead of 1,000. This single
      design choice lets you run a commercial app on a free or $19/mo plan.                                                    
  2. Display Snippets & Deep Link (Copyright Compliance):                                                                      
      • Low-cost news APIs grant redistribution rights for metadata (headlines, 200–300 character descriptions, publish dates, 
      thumbnails, and canonical URLs).                                                                                         
      • Do not scrape and republish complete full-text articles without explicit syndication agreements (e.g., direct with     
      Reuters or AP), as that creates copyright liability.                                                                     
  3. Recommended Hybrid Stack:                                                                                                 
      • Use TheNewsAPI ($0 to $19/mo) for general geopolitics and world news.                                                  
      • Use Marketaux ($0 to $29/mo) or Alpha Vantage ($0 to $24.99/mo) for stock ticker sentiment and financial markets.
