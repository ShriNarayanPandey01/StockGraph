    [08] BEFORE YOU EXPLORETHE IMPORTANT DISTINCTIONS

    Context, clearly.

    Is the market data live?+

    The interactive workspace requests current company, candle, and graph data from the Data and Graph services. Each numeric market value includes its vendor and as-of timestamp. Some landing-page walkthroughs remain illustrative and are labelled as examples.

    Does a link explain a price move?+

    A link shows a relationship or relevant context. It does not prove cause or quantify a price effect. Evidence and time windows help you assess the connection.

    Are stock chat and trading bots available?+

    Research chat, discovery scans, monitors, and opportunity alerts are available in Phase 1 and are processed by a separately hosted agent. Results may remain queued until that agent is online. Broker connections and live order execution are deferred.

    What can I try right now?+

    Open the live workspace, search a stock, review its graph connections, and inspect available source evidence. You can also try the sample stock-chat questions, preview the bot workflow, and watch the 18-second motion study.
