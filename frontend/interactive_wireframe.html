Architectural Foundations

Built for Truth. Engineered Against Hallucination.

Standard vector embeddings fail on equities because semantic proximity does not equal economic causality. StockGraph enforces four non-negotiable architectural layers:

01 / FIRST-ORDER FACTS TimescaleDB Primary

Official Exchange Bhavcopy & Reg-30 Ground Truth

Never rely on scraped or approximate trading volume. StockGraph uses official NSE Bhavcopy (sec_bhavdata_full.csv) as the immutable source of truth for Delivery % and institutional turnover, computing 20-day delivery z-scores mathematically in SQL.

2 Checkpoints/Day (OPEN/CLOSE) Bulk/Block CSV Deals SEBI SAST Insiders
02 / SECOND-ORDER TRANSMISSION DynamicOntologyBuilder

Dynamic LLM Ontology with Context Injection

Static hand-coded graphs rot overnight. When an LLM evaluates stock relationships, StockGraph injects a rich StockContextCard (business model, raw materials, revenue segments, and concall guidance). The LLM determines margin sensitivity with enforced confidence floors (≥ 0.65).

[:EXPOSED_TO] [:SENSITIVE_TO_MACRO] [:RELATED_TO] Commodities
03 / MULTI-DAY NARRATIVE CHAINS NewsCorrelator (L1-L4)

Four-Layer Deduplication & Cross-News Correlation

Unfolding market stories arrive across multiple days from different providers. StockGraph executes L1 URL deduplication, L2 headline normalization, L3 vector deduplication (cosine ≥ 0.92), and L4 causal chaining ([:CORRELATED_WITH]) to reconstruct full market narrative arcs.

Deterministic SHA-1 IDs 14-Day Trailing Window CAUSAL_CHAIN Edges
04 / BI-TEMPORAL DOSSIER RAG Fixed Windows Only

Strict Temporal Filtering & Superseding Nodes

Standard RAG suffers from "temporal collapse" (retrieving old Q1 guidance alongside Q3). StockGraph queries are strictly bounded to window=LAST_6M | LAST_1Y. Outdated quarterly ratios rotate with [:SUPERSEDES] links, ensuring your agent views the exact state of reality.
