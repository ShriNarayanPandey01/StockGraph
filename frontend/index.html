    [09] BEFORE YOU EXPLORETHE IMPORTANT DISTINCTIONS

    Context, clearly.

    Are prices and news streaming live?+

    No. The target backend uses scheduled collection, with news ingestion hourly and market records on scheduled cadences. The landing page uses dated historical context and illustrative numbers, labeled with their status.

    Does a graph link prove a price move?+

    No. Direct and indirect links explain a relationship, source and rationale. They do not prove causation. Confidence is shown when available, with dates and competing evidence nearby.

    Are chat and discovery running?+

    Research chat, discovery and monitoring are implemented as an opt-in developer pilot. Availability depends on the worker, evidence services and configured budget.

    Can StockGraph place trades?+

    No. Placing trades and automated trading are out of scope. Upstox is for market-data requests; portfolio access and execution are not enabled.

    How do I get Pro access?+

    The developer assigns Free or Pro manually, with optional expiry. There is no working subscription, payment or checkout flow.

    What can I explore?+

    Search the catalog, select stocks, explore available connections and manage your watchlists. Access depends on your account and the availability of the underlying services.
